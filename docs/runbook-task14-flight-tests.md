# Task 14 Runbook - Flight Tests (open area, then balloon-pop)

Progressive, stop-if-anything-looks-wrong flight validation. A second
person spotting, and a GoPro/phone recording every flight, is strongly
recommended for post-flight review against the checklist below.

## (a) Open-area hop, no target present

- Normal manual takeoff.
- At a safe altitude, flip the lock-on switch on with no balloon in view.
- Confirm: plane keeps flying on the pilot's sticks (searching state,
  passthrough), the Zorro shows no lock indicator, and flipping the
  switch off again changes nothing (nothing was ever locked).
- Separately, deliberately fly down near (but safely above) the 15ft
  floor with the switch on - confirm the plane climbs back up on its own
  before actually reaching 15ft actual AGL, with enough margin for your
  specific descent rate and the floor's recovery-margin setting.
- Land manually.

## (b) Supervised pursuit of a tethered/held balloon

- A second person holds (or tethers, at a safe stand-off distance and
  height) a balloon in an open area clear of people/obstacles.
- Pilot flies a pass, flips the lock-on switch on once roughly pointed at
  the balloon, and is ready to flip it off at any time.
- Confirm: lock acquired (indicator on Zorro), plane turns/climbs toward
  the balloon with visible lead on a moving/swinging balloon, pilot can
  reclaim control instantly at any point by flipping the switch.
- Repeat a few times, varying balloon distance/angle, before moving on.

## (c) Balloon-pop test flight

- Same setup as (b), but let the engagement run through to contact.
- Confirm on the recording: switch-triggered engagement, the 15ft floor
  held throughout, lock acquired and indicated on the Zorro, pursuit
  flown with predictive lead, contact made, and that pilot override was
  available (and unused, or used, either way confirmed available) for the
  whole engagement.

Review every flight's recording against this checklist before calling
the project done - don't rely on in-the-moment impressions alone.
